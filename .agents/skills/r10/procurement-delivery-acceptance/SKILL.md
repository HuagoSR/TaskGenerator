# Procurement delivery acceptance

## Purpose

Use this Skill to shape a credible post-delivery commercial acquisition situation. The contracting officer's representative is the primary working role; the contracting officer and receiving official are natural decision, authorization, and review audiences. Keep inspection, receipt, recommendation, and contractual acceptance distinct when the facts or delegation require it.

## Situation frame

Trigger the work when commercial products or services have been tendered and the receiving team needs an acceptance disposition, but the record must first be reconciled to contract requirements and quality evidence.

Use natural contract and source-system materials such as relevant award terms, shipment notices, delivery tickets, receiving-system entries, contractor certificates, inspection records, quality-system evidence, discrepancy notices, communications, and role-delegation records. Retain natural authors, dates, identifiers, and differing purposes. A receiving entry may establish physical receipt without proving conformity or authorized acceptance.

## Judgment to elicit

Require the candidate to connect the tendered product or service and available quality evidence to governing requirements, identify discrepancies, and create a reviewable disposition or follow-up record. The evidence may support acceptance, a hold, referral, or another authorized response, but delivery alone must not decide the issue.

Plausible gaps include missing or inconsistent quantities, unclear model or service scope, absent quality records, unverifiable signatures, conflicting dates, condition issues, an unresolved modification, evidence tied to the wrong line item, uncertainty about acceptance authority, and nonconformance lacking an authorized disposition. Leave the candidate to decide whether the record supports acceptance, who must act, and what follow-up remains visible.

## Construction boundaries

Do not encode a disposition in filenames, headings, answer labels, discrepancy language, or reviewer comments. Do not prescribe a candidate procedure sequence, a teacher solution path, or a fixed log layout. Do not invent a policy that overrides the contract or FAR authority.
