# Source map

## FAR 46.202-1

Official source: https://www.acquisition.gov/far/46.202-1. It constrains commercial-product quality assurance and appropriate use of a contractor quality system, without making contractor evidence automatically sufficient or supplying product, supplier, or conformity facts.

## FAR 46.407

Official source: https://www.acquisition.gov/far/46.407, especially paragraphs (a)–(c). It constrains nonconformance handling to an authorized, documented disposition rather than informal acceptance for convenience.

## FAR 46.501 and FAR 46.503

Official sources: https://www.acquisition.gov/far/46.501 and https://www.acquisition.gov/far/46.503. They distinguish authorized acceptance from receipt or inspection and support realistic discrepancies between shipment, receipt, inspection, and acceptance records without prescribing an outcome.
