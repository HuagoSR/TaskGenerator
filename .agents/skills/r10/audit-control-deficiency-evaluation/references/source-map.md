# Source map

## PCAOB AS 2201

Official source: https://pcaobus.org/oversight/standards/auditing-standards/details/AS2201, especially paragraphs .62–.70. It constrains the scenario to an integrated-audit deficiency evaluation grounded in reasonable possibility and potential magnitude, with deficiencies considered individually and in combination. It supplies evaluation criteria, not scenario facts or a predetermined classification.

## PCAOB AS 1215

Official source: https://pcaobus.org/oversight/standards/auditing-standards/details/AS1215, especially paragraphs .06–.10. It supports a reviewable rationale that records work performed, evidence obtained, conclusions reached, and identifying characteristics without requiring a fixed workpaper template.

## PCAOB inspections

Official source: https://pcaobus.org/oversight/inspections. Inspection materials provide failure-mode context for unsupported conclusions, incomplete treatment of related deficiencies, and premature reliance on remediation; they must not be copied into scenario facts or expected answers.
