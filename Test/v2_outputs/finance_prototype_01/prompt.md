### Audit Assignment: 2024 Fall Music Tour Reconciliation

**Role:** Senior Auditor

**Engagement Context:**
The client operated an international music tour and prepared a partially structured audit workbook with separate tabs for tour-manager revenue, withholding-tax assumptions, and production-company costs. Reporting is being completed in January 2025 for an as-of date of December 31, 2024.

Management needs a completed profit-and-loss workbook that rolls the provided support tabs into one coherent audit-ready summary. Your work should preserve the workbook structure, complete the required calculations, and keep the final result suitable for executive review.

**Objective:**
Produce a consolidated cross-source profit and loss report for executive review.

**Working Expectations:**
- Use the attached reference files as the sole working data sources.
- Complete a final P&L workbook that shows Tour Manager, Production Company, and Total columns.
- Report revenues in USD and apply withholding-tax adjustments before presenting net income.
- If the source materials contain irregularities or incomplete information, resolve them carefully inside the workbook logic.

**Required Deliverables:**
1. Create an Excel workbook named `fall_music_tour_output.xlsx`.
2. Include a clear header stating `As of 12/31/2024`.
3. The workbook must present gross revenue, withholding taxes, total costs, and final net income.
4. The workbook should be professionally structured and readable without adding unrelated files.

**Quality Bar:**
The final package should read like a real client-facing audit work product: numerically coherent, professionally formatted, and decision-ready.
