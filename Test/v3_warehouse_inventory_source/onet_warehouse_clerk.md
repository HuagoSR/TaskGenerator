# Shipping, Receiving, and Inventory Clerk — occupation evidence

Source publisher: O*NET OnLine, U.S. Department of Labor sponsored program.
Source URL: https://www.onetonline.org/link/details/43-5071.00
Retrieved: 2026-07-10.
Use: short attributed excerpt and structured paraphrase for public-source skill extraction.

The occupation verifies and maintains records for incoming and outgoing shipments involving inventory. Core recordkeeping work includes examining shipment contents and comparing them with manifests, invoices, or orders to verify accuracy.

Workers record shipment data such as quantity, weight, charges, damage, and discrepancies. They prepare shipping or work-order documentation and correspond with relevant parties to rectify shortages, damage, wrong items, or other nonconformance.

Reusable workflow: compare expected shipment records with the receiving record; preserve shipment and item identifiers; classify each difference; update inventory only for accepted quantities; report unresolved exceptions to an operations supervisor.
